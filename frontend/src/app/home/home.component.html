DDMed Platform

Clinical workflows, terminology tooling and interoperability in one studio

DDMed Clinical Studio is a modular workspace for teams building computable healthcare products. It brings together clinical data entry, terminology operations, analytics, questionnaires and exchange scenarios in a single product surface designed for implementation work rather than screenshots.

The modules below remain transparent and reusable, but the experience is now framed as a cohesive product: a practical environment for architects, implementers, clinicians and informatics teams working with FHIR, SNOMED CT, LOINC and related standards.
